# Disco Inferno

A Warhammer 40,000: Darktide mod. When the Skitarii Purgator's servo skull goes empowered, it blasts heavy metal and fires two strobing spotlights from its eyes that ride the skull's aim.

Requires [SimpleAudio](https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide). Drop your own tracks into the mod's `audio/` folder (mp3/ogg/wav and more). No audio is bundled.

## Per-song settings

Open the in-game panel with the `/di` chat command or the "Show settings window" toggle in the mod options. For each track you can set:

- Music volume
- Strobe tempo (BPM) and brightness
- Rainbow mode, or a separate colour for each eye (left / right)

Settings are saved per track and applied live while a disco is playing.

## Commands

- `/di` - open or close the settings window.
- `/di_test [off]` - force the disco on your own servo skull for testing.
